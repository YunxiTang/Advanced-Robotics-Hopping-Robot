| sole contacts left / right | 4 / 4 | 4 / 4 (foot flat) |
| max abs force error | 0.004519 | < 0.16 N (2% of the largest component) |
| max abs moment error | 2.579e-05 | < 0.0050 N m (5% of the largest component) |

A sign error here shows up as a force of the wrong sign, or no contact at all; a moment about the wrong point shows up as a moment error proportional to the force. Without the joint-damping feedforward the torsion comes out 25-33% short: MuJoCo's soft friction lets the foot creep in yaw at 0.05 rad/s under a steady twist, and the hip yaw's 0.2 damping eats 0.01 N m of it. (With the old point foot, the same test caught a Jacobian taken at the foot sphere's centre instead of its contact point: 11% too little horizontal force.)
